The mass of a 73Li nucleus is 0.042 u less than the sum of the masses of all its nucleons. The average binding energy per nucleon of 73 Li nucleus is nearly

```markdown
The total binding energy of a nucleus is

$$
\text{B.E.} = \Delta m \times 931.5\ \text{MeV}
$$

Given,

$$
\Delta m = 0.042\ \text{u}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\text{B.E.} = 0.042 \times 931.5
= 39.12\ \text{MeV}
$$

For the nucleus ${}^{7}_{3}\mathrm{Li}$,

$$
A = 7
$$

Average binding energy per nucleon is

$$
\frac{\text{B.E.}}{A}
=
\frac{39.12}{7}
=
5.59\ \text{MeV}
$$

$$
\approx 5.6\ \text{MeV}
$$

Hence, **the average binding energy per nucleon is \(5.6\ \text{MeV}\).**
```
