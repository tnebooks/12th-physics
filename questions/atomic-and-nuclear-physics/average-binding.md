**The mass of a ⁷₃Li nucleus is 0.042 u less than the sum of the masses of all its nucleons. The average binding energy per nucleon of ⁷₃Li nucleus is nearly**

```markdown
Given:
- Mass defect, Δm = 0.042 u
- 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c² (mass-energy conversion factor)
- Total number of nucleons in ⁷₃Li, A = 7

Total binding energy:
E_b = Δm × 931.5 = 0.042 × 931.5 = 39.123 MeV

Binding energy per nucleon:
E_b_per_nucleon = E_b / A = 39.123 / 7 = 5.589 ≈ **5.6 MeV**

Therefore, the average binding energy per nucleon of ⁷₃Li nucleus is nearly **5.6 MeV**.
```
