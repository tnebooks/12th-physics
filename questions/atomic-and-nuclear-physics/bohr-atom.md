**The electric potential of an electron is given by V = V₀ ln(r/r₀) where r₀ is a constant. If Bohr atom model is valid, then variation of radius of $n^{\text{th}}$ orbit rₙ with the principal quantum number n is**

```markdown
Given: Potential energy of electron, V(r) = V₀ ln(r/r₀)

Force on the electron:  
F = -dV/dr = -V₀/r  
Magnitude of force, F = V₀/r

For circular orbit in Bohr model, this force provides the centripetal force:  
V₀/r = mv²/r  →  mv² = V₀  →  v = √(V₀/m)  (constant)

So, the speed v is **constant** for all orbits.

Now, Bohr's quantization condition:  
mvr = nℏ

Since m and v are constants:  
r ∝ n

Therefore, the radius of the nth orbit varies as:  
**rₙ ∝ n**

Correct answer: **rₙ ∝ n**
```
