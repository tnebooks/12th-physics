The electric potential of an electron is given by V = V∘ ln( r/r∘) where r0 is a constant. If Bohr atom model is valid, then variation of radius of nth orbit rn with the principal quantum number n is

```markdown
According to Bohr's quantization condition,

$$
mvr=\frac{nh}{2\pi}
$$

The given potential is

$$
V(r)=V_0\ln\left(\frac{r}{r_0}\right)
$$

Hence, the force is

$$
F=-\frac{dV}{dr}
=
-\frac{V_0}{r}
$$

Thus,

$$
F\propto \frac{1}{r}
$$

For circular motion,

$$
\frac{mv^2}{r}=F
$$

Therefore,

$$
\frac{mv^2}{r}\propto \frac{1}{r}
\Rightarrow v=\text{constant}
$$

Using Bohr's condition,

$$
mvr\propto n
$$

Since $v$ is constant,

$$
r_n\propto n
$$

Hence,

$$
\boxed{r_n\propto n}
$$
```
