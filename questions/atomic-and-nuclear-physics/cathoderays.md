The charge of cathode rays particle is

```markdown
Cathode rays are streams of fast-moving electrons emitted from the cathode in a discharge tube.

Since electrons carry a negative electric charge, cathode rays are negatively charged.

Hence, **the charge of cathode ray particles is negative.**
```
