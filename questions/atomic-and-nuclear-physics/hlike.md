**Atomic number of H-like atom with ionization potential 122.4 V for n = 1 is**

```markdown
For a hydrogen-like atom, the energy of the electron in the ground state (n = 1) is given by:

E₁ = -13.6 × Z² eV

Ionization potential is the energy required to remove the electron from n = 1 to n = ∞, which is equal to |E₁|.

Given ionization potential = 122.4 V (which is 122.4 eV):

13.6 × Z² = 122.4  
Z² = 122.4 / 13.6 = 9  
Z = √9 = 3

Therefore, the atomic number Z = **3**.

Correct answer: **3**
```
