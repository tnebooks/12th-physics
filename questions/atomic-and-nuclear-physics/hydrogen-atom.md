**In a hydrogen atom, the electron revolving in the fourth orbit, has angular momentum equal to**

```markdown
According to Bohr's postulates, the angular momentum of an electron in the nth orbit is given by:

L = nℏ = n(h/2π)

For the fourth orbit, n = 4:

L = 4 × (h/2π) = 4h/2π = 2h/π

Therefore, the angular momentum of the electron in the fourth orbit is **2h/π**.

Correct answer: **2h/π**
```
