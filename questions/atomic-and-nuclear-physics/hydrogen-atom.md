In a hydrogen atom, the electron revolving in the fourth orbit, has angular momentum equal to

```markdown
According to Bohr's quantization condition, the angular momentum of an electron in the $n$th orbit is

$$
L=mvr=\frac{nh}{2\pi}
$$

For the fourth orbit,

$$
n=4
$$

Therefore,

$$
L=\frac{4h}{2\pi}
=\frac{2h}{\pi}
$$

Hence, the angular momentum of the electron in the fourth orbit is

$$
\boxed{\frac{2h}{\pi}}
$$
```
