    - "1.7 × 10^11 C kg^–1"

  In J.J. Thomson e/m experiment, electrons are accelerated through 2.6 kV enter the region of crossed electric field and magnetic field of strength 3.0 × 10^4 Vm^–1 and 1.0 × 10^–3 T respectively, and pass through it and undeflected, then the specific charge is

```markdown
For an electron passing undeflected through crossed electric and magnetic fields,

$$
eE=evB
$$

Hence,

$$
v=\frac{E}{B}
=\frac{3.0\times10^4}{1.0\times10^{-3}}
=3.0\times10^7\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}
$$

The accelerating potential is

$$
V=2.6\times10^3\,\mathrm{V}
$$

Using

$$
eV=\frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$

we get

$$
\frac{e}{m}
=
\frac{v^2}{2V}
=
\frac{(3.0\times10^7)^2}{2(2.6\times10^3)}
\approx1.73\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{C\,kg^{-1}}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{1.7\times10^{11}\,\mathrm{C\,kg^{-1}}}
$$
```
