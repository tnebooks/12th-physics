**A radioactive nucleus (initial mass number A and atomic number Z) emits two α-particles and 2 positrons. The ratio of number of neutrons to that of proton in the final nucleus will be**

```markdown
Initial nucleus:
- Mass number = A
- Atomic number = Z
- Neutrons = A - Z
- Protons = Z

**Emission of 2 α-particles (⁴₂He):**
- Mass number decreases by: 2 × 4 = 8
- Atomic number decreases by: 2 × 2 = 4
- New mass number = A - 8
- New atomic number = Z - 4
- Protons = Z - 4
- Neutrons = (A - 8) - (Z - 4) = A - Z - 4

**Emission of 2 positrons (⁰₊₁e):**
- Each positron emission converts a proton into a neutron
- For each positron: Z decreases by 1, A remains same
- For 2 positrons: Z decreases by 2, A unchanged
- New atomic number = (Z - 4) - 2 = Z - 6
- Mass number = A - 8 (unchanged)
- Protons = Z - 6
- Neutrons = (A - 8) - (Z - 6) = A - Z - 2

Final nucleus:
- Neutrons : Protons = (A - Z - 2) : (Z - 6)

Ratio = **(A - Z - 2) / (Z - 6)**

Therefore, the correct answer is: **A-Z-2/Z-6**
```
