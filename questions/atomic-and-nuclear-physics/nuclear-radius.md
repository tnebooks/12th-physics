If the nuclear radius of ²⁷Al is 3.6 fermi,the approximate nuclear radius of ⁶⁴Cu in fermi is

```markdown
The radius of a nucleus is given by

$$
R = R_0 A^{1/3}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\frac{R_{Cu}}{R_{Al}}
=
\left(\frac{64}{27}\right)^{1/3}
=
\frac{4}{3}
$$

Given the radius of $^{27}\mathrm{Al}$ nucleus is

$$
R_{Al}=3.6\ \text{fm}
$$

Hence,

$$
R_{Cu}
=
3.6 \times \frac{4}{3}
=
4.8\ \text{fm}
$$

Therefore, the correct answer is

$$
\boxed{4.8\ \text{fm}}
$$
```
