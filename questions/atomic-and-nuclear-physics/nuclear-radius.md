**If the nuclear radius of ²⁷Al is 3.6 fermi, the approximate nuclear radius of ⁶⁴Cu in fermi is**

```markdown
Nuclear radius is given by:  
R = R₀ A^(1/3)

Given:  
For ²⁷Al, A₁ = 27, R₁ = 3.6 fermi  
For ⁶⁴Cu, A₂ = 64, R₂ = ?

Using the relation:  
R₂ / R₁ = (A₂ / A₁)^(1/3)

R₂ / 3.6 = (64 / 27)^(1/3)  
64^(1/3) = 4  
27^(1/3) = 3  
R₂ / 3.6 = 4/3  
R₂ = 3.6 × (4/3) = 3.6 × 1.333 = 4.8 fermi

Therefore, the nuclear radius of ⁶⁴Cu is **4.8 fermi**.

Correct answer: **4.8**
```
