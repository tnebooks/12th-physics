A radiative element has N∘ number of nuclei at t=0. The number of nuclei remaining after half of a half-life (that is, at time t=1/2Tof1/2)

```markdown
Given,

$$
t=\frac{T_{1/2}}{2}
$$

The radioactive decay law is

$$
N=N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/T_{1/2}}
$$

Substituting

$$
t=\frac{T_{1/2}}{2}
$$

we get

$$
N=N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{1/2}
$$

$$
N=\frac{N_0}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

Therefore, the number of nuclei remaining is

$$
\boxed{\frac{N_0}{\sqrt{2}}}
$$
```
