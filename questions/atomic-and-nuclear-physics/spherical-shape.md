**The nucleus is approximately spherical in shape. Then the surface area of nucleus having mass number $A$ varies as**

```markdown
The nuclear radius is given by the formula:
$$R = R_0 A^{1/3}$$

Since the nucleus is approximately spherical, its surface area is:
$$S = 4\pi R^2$$

Substituting the value of $R$ into the area formula:
$$S = 4\pi \left( R_0 A^{1/3} \right)^2$$
$$S = 4\pi R_0^2 A^{2/3}$$

Therefore, the surface area $S$ is directly proportional to $A^{2/3}$.

Correct answer is **$A^{2/3}$**.

```
