The nucleus is approximately spherical in shape. Then the surface area of nucleus having mass number A varies as

```markdown
For a nucleus,

$$
R = R_0 A^{1/3}
$$

The surface area of a spherical nucleus is

$$
S = 4\pi R^2
$$

Substituting $R \propto A^{1/3}$,

$$
S \propto \left(A^{1/3}\right)^2
$$

$$
S \propto A^{2/3}
$$

Therefore, the surface area of the nucleus varies as

$$
\boxed{A^{2/3}}
$$
```
