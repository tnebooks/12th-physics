**The ratio between the radius of first three orbits of hydrogen atom is**

```markdown
In a hydrogen atom, the radius of the nth orbit is given by:

rₙ ∝ n²

Therefore,
r₁ : r₂ : r₃ = 1² : 2² : 3² = 1 : 4 : 9

So, the ratio between the radii of the first three orbits is **1 : 4 : 9**.

Correct answer: **1:4:9**
```
