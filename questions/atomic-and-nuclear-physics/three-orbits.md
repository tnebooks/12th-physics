The ratio between the radius of first three orbits of hydrogen atom is

```markdown
According to Bohr's model, the radius of the $n^{\text{th}}$ orbit is

$$
r_n = n^2 a_0
$$

where $a_0$ is the Bohr radius.

For the first three orbits:

$$
r_1 = 1^2 a_0 = a_0
$$

$$
r_2 = 2^2 a_0 = 4a_0
$$

$$
r_3 = 3^2 a_0 = 9a_0
$$

Therefore,

$$
r_1 : r_2 : r_3 = 1 : 4 : 9
$$

Hence, the correct answer is

$$
\boxed{1:4:9}
$$
```
