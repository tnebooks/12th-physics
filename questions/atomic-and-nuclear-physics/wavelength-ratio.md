**The ratio of the wavelengths of radiation emitted for the transition from n = 2 to n = 1 in Li⁺⁺, He⁺ and H is**

```markdown
For a hydrogen-like atom, the wavelength (λ) of emitted radiation for transition from n₂ to n₁ is given by:

1/λ = R Z² (1/n₁² - 1/n₂²)

For n₁ = 1 and n₂ = 2:

1/λ = R Z² (1/1² - 1/2²) = R Z² (1 - 1/4) = (3/4) R Z²

So, λ ∝ 1/Z²

Given:
- H (Hydrogen): Z = 1
- He⁺ (Singly ionized helium): Z = 2
- Li⁺⁺ (Doubly ionized lithium): Z = 3

Therefore:
λ_H : λ_He⁺ : λ_Li⁺⁺ = 1/1² : 1/2² : 1/3² = 1 : 1/4 : 1/9

Multiplying by 36 (LCM):
λ_H : λ_He⁺ : λ_Li⁺⁺ = 36 : 9 : 4

But the question asks for Li⁺⁺ : He⁺ : H (reverse order):
λ_Li⁺⁺ : λ_He⁺ : λ_H = 4 : 9 : 36

Therefore, the correct ratio is **4 : 9 : 36**.

Correct answer: **4:9:36**
```
