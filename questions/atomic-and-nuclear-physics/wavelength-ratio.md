The ratio of the wavelengths radiation emitted for the transition from n =2 to n = 1 in Li^++, He^+ and H is

```markdown
For hydrogen-like atoms,

$$
\frac{1}{\lambda}
=
RZ^2
\left(
\frac{1}{1^2}
-
\frac{1}{2^2}
\right)
$$

For the transition $n=2 \rightarrow n=1$, the term

$$
\left(
\frac{1}{1^2}
-
\frac{1}{2^2}
\right)
$$

is constant.

Hence,

$$
\frac{1}{\lambda}\propto Z^2
$$

Therefore,

$$
\lambda \propto \frac{1}{Z^2}
$$

For the ions:

- $Li^{2+}: Z=3$
- $He^{+}: Z=2$
- $H: Z=1$

So,

$$
\lambda_{Li^{2+}}:\lambda_{He^{+}}:\lambda_H
=
\frac{1}{9}:\frac{1}{4}:1
$$

Multiplying by $36$,

$$
\lambda_{Li^{2+}}:\lambda_{He^{+}}:\lambda_H
=
4:9:36
$$

Hence, the correct answer is

$$
\boxed{4:9:36}
$$
```
