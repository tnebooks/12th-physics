In the combination of the given logic gates, what is the Boolean equation for output $Y$ in terms of inputs $A$, $B$, and $C$?

```markdown
Passing input $A$ through a NOT gate yields $\bar{A}$. Combining $\bar{A}$, $B$, and $C$ through an OR gate results in the output expression:
$$Y = \bar{A} + B + C$$
```
