If a material having intensity of magnetisation $500\text{ A m}^{-1}$ is placed in a magnetising field of $1000\text{ A m}^{-1}$, then the magnetic susceptibility ($\chi_m$) of the material is:

```markdown
Magnetic susceptibility is defined as the ratio of the intensity of magnetisation ($M$) to the magnetising field intensity ($H$):
$$\chi_m = \frac{M}{H} = \frac{500\text{ A m}^{-1}}{1000\text{ A m}^{-1}} = 0.5$$
```
