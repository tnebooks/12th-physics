In a transformer, the number of turns in the primary and secondary coils are $410$ and $1230$ respectively. If the current in the primary coil is $6\text{ A}$, then the current in the secondary coil is:

```markdown
For an ideal transformer, the relation between currents and number of turns is:
$$\frac{I_s}{I_p} = \frac{N_p}{N_s}$$

Substituting the given values:
$$I_s = I_p \times \frac{N_p}{N_s} = 6 \times \frac{410}{1230} = 6 \times \frac{1}{3} = 2\text{ A}$$
```
