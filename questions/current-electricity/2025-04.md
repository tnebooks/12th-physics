In Joule’s heating law ($H = I^2 R t$), when $R$ and $t$ are constant, if $H$ is taken along the y-axis and $I^2$ along the x-axis, the graph is:

```markdown
According to Joule's law of heating, $H = I^2 R t$. 

When resistance $R$ and time $t$ are constants, $H \propto I^2$. Comparing this to the equation of a straight line passing through the origin ($y = mx$), where $y = H$, $x = I^2$, and slope $m = Rt$, the plot is a **straight line**.
```
