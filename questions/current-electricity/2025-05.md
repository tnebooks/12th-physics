The dimension of $\frac{1}{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}$ is:

```markdown
The speed of light in vacuum is related to permeability and permittivity by:
$$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}} \implies \frac{1}{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0} = c^2$$

Since the dimensional formula for velocity $c$ is $[\text{L}\text{T}^{-1}]$:
$$\left[\frac{1}{\mu_0 \varepsilon_0}\right] = \left[\text{L}\text{T}^{-1}\right]^2 = \left[\text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-2}\right]$$
```
