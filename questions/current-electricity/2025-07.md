An electric dipole is placed at an alignment angle of $30^\circ$ with an electric field of $2 \times 10^5\text{ N C}^{-1}$. It experiences a torque equal to $8\text{ N m}$. The charge on the dipole if the dipole length is $1\text{ cm}$ is:

```markdown
The torque experienced by a dipole in a uniform electric field is given by:
$$\tau = p E \sin\theta = (q \cdot 2a) E \sin\theta$$

Given:
- $\tau = 8\text{ N m}$
- $2a = 1\text{ cm} = 10^{-2}\text{ m}$
- $E = 2 \times 10^5\text{ N C}^{-1}$
- $\theta = 30^\circ \implies \sin 30^\circ = 0.5$

Substitute into the equation:
$$8 = q \times 10^{-2} \times (2 \times 10^5) \times 0.5$$
$$8 = q \times 10^3 \implies q = \frac{8}{10^3}\text{ C} = 8\text{ mC}$$
```
