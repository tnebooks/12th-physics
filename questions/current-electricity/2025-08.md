For light incident from air on a slab of refractive index $2$, the maximum possible angle of refraction is:

```markdown
By Snell's law:
$$n_1 \sin i = n_2 \sin r$$

For light entering from air ($n_1 = 1$) into a medium of refractive index $n_2 = 2$:
$$\sin r = \frac{\sin i}{2}$$

The maximum angle of incidence is $i_{\max} = 90^\circ$ ($\sin 90^\circ = 1$), hence:
$$\sin r_{\max} = \frac{1}{2} \implies r_{\max} = 30^\circ$$
```
