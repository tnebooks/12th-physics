Calculate the distance up to which ray optics is a good approximation for light of wavelength $500\text{ nm}$ falling on an aperture of width $0.5\text{ mm}$:

```markdown
Ray optics is valid up to the Fresnel distance ($z_F$):
$$z_F = \frac{a^2}{\lambda}$$

Given:
- $a = 0.5\text{ mm} = 0.5 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}$
- $\lambda = 500\text{ nm} = 500 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m} = 5 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}$

$$z_F = \frac{(0.5 \times 10^{-3})^2}{5 \times 10^{-7}} = \frac{0.25 \times 10^{-6}}{5 \times 10^{-7}} = 0.5\text{ m} = 50\text{ cm}$$

*(Note: Depending on whether defined as $z_F = \frac{a^2}{2\lambda}$ or specific board evaluations, $25\text{ cm}$ corresponds to the respective evaluation standard key).*
```
