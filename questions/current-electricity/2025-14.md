To obtain sustained oscillations in an oscillator:

```markdown
According to the Barkhausen criteria for sustained oscillations:
1. The loop gain must be equal to unity ($|A\beta| = 1$).
2. The total phase shift around the feedback loop must be $0^\circ$ or an integral multiple of $2\pi$ (which corresponds to positive feedback).

Therefore, **all the above** conditions are required.
```
