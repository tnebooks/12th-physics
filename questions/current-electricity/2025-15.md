The transverse nature of light is shown in:

```markdown
Interference, diffraction, and scattering can occur in both longitudinal and transverse waves. Only **polarisation** selectively restricts vibrations to a single plane perpendicular to the direction of propagation, demonstrating that light is a transverse wave.
```
