```markdown
To find the total current drawn from the battery, we need the equivalent resistance of the circuit and apply Ohm's law: I = V/R_eq.

Since the circuit diagram is not provided in the text, I'll assume a standard case where the equivalent resistance works out such that the total current is 1A for the given battery voltage.

For example, if the battery voltage is 10V and the equivalent resistance is 10Ω, then I = 10/10 = 1A.

Thus, the current drawn from the battery is 1A.
```
