The internal resistance of a 2.1 V cell which gives a current of 0.2 A through a resistance of 10 Ω is

```markdown
Here's the short explanation:

- Total voltage = 2.1 V  
- Current = 0.2 A  
- External resistance = 10 Ω  

Voltage across external resistor:  
V = I × R = 0.2 × 10 = 2.0 V  

Remaining voltage is dropped across the internal resistance:  
2.1 − 2.0 = 0.1 V  

Internal resistance = Voltage drop / Current  
= 0.1 / 0.2 = 0.5 Ω  

**Final answer:** 0.5 Ω
```
