**In Joule’s heating law, when R and t are constant, if the H is taken along the y axis and I² along the x axis, the graph is**

```markdown
Joule's heating law states:  
**H = I² R t**

Here, R and t are constants, so we can write:  
H = (R t) × I²

Let y = H and x = I². Then the equation becomes:  
y = (R t) × x

Since (R t) is a constant, this is of the form y = mx, which represents a **straight line** passing through the origin.

Therefore, the graph is a **straight line**.
```
