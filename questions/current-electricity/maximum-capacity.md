**In a large building, there are 15 bulbs of 40 W, 5 bulbs of 100 W, 5 fans of 80 W and 1 heater of 1kW are connected. The voltage of electric mains is 220 V. The maximum capacity of the main fuse of the building will be**

```markdown
First, calculate the total power consumption:

- 15 bulbs of 40 W → 15 × 40 = 600 W
- 5 bulbs of 100 W → 5 × 100 = 500 W
- 5 fans of 80 W → 5 × 80 = 400 W
- 1 heater of 1 kW → 1000 × 1 = 1000 W

Total power P = 600 + 500 + 400 + 1000 = 2500 W

Current I = P / V = 2500 / 220 = 11.36 ≈ 12 A

Therefore, the maximum current capacity of the main fuse should be **12 A**.
```
