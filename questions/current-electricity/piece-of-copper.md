**A piece of copper and another of germanium are cooled from room temperature to 80 K. The resistance of**

```markdown
- **Copper** is a metal. For metals, resistance **decreases** as temperature decreases (because electron scattering reduces).

- **Germanium** is a semiconductor. For semiconductors, resistance **increases** as temperature decreases (because the number of charge carriers reduces).

Therefore, when cooled from room temperature to 80 K:

- Resistance of copper **decreases**
- Resistance of germanium **increases**

Correct answer: **copper decreases and germanium increases**
```
