Two wires A and B with circular cross section are made up of the same material with equal lengths. Suppose RA = 3RB, then what is the ratio of radius of wire A to that of wire B?

```markdown
Since R = ρL/(πr²), resistance is inversely proportional to the square of radius. Thus R_A/R_B = r_B²/r_A² = 3, giving r_A/r_B = 1/√3.
```
