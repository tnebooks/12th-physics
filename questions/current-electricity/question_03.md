A non-conducting charged ring carrying a charge q, mass m and radius r is rotated about its axis with constant angular speed ω. The ratio of its magnetic moment to angular momentum is:

```markdown
For a rotating charged ring, magnetic moment μ = qωr²/2 and angular momentum L = mωr². Hence μ/L = q/(2m).
```
