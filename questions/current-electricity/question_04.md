When the current changes from +2 A to -2 A in 0.05 s, an emf of 8 V is induced in a coil. The coefficient of self induction of the coil is:

```markdown
Using |ε| = L|ΔI/Δt|, ΔI = 4 A. Therefore L = 8×0.05/4 = 0.1 H.
```
