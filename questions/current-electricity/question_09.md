The mass of a 7Li nucleus is 0.042 u less than the sum of the masses of all its nucleons. The average binding energy per nucleon of 7Li nucleus is nearly:

```markdown
Total binding energy = Δm×931 MeV = 0.042×931 ≈ 39.1 MeV. Per nucleon = 39.1/7 ≈ 5.6 MeV.
```
