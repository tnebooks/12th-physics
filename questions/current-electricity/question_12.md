The potential energy of a magnetic dipole whose dipole moment is Pm = (-0.5i + 0.4j) Am² kept in uniform magnetic field B = 2j T is:

```markdown
Potential energy U = −μ·B. Here μ·B = (−0.5i + 0.4j)·2j = 0.8, so U = −0.8 J.
```
