**A wire connected to a power supply of 230 V has power dissipation P₁. Suppose the wire is cut into two equal pieces and connected parallel to the same power supply. In this case power dissipation is P₂. The ratio P₂/P₁ is**

```markdown
Let the original resistance of the wire be R.

Power P₁ = V² / R  (where V = 230 V)

When the wire is cut into two equal pieces, each piece has resistance = R/2.

When these two pieces are connected in parallel, the equivalent resistance R_eq is:
1/R_eq = 1/(R/2) + 1/(R/2) = 2/R + 2/R = 4/R  
R_eq = R/4

New power P₂ = V² / (R/4) = 4V²/R = 4P₁

Therefore, P₂/P₁ = 4/1 = **4**
```
