**There is a current of 1.0 A in the circuit shown below. What is the resistance of P?**

```markdown
Since the circuit diagram is not provided in the query, I'll explain the general method to find the unknown resistance P.

Given:
- Total current in the circuit, I = 1.0 A
- Supply voltage, V (assume from the circuit)
- Other known resistances in series/parallel

Using Ohm's law:  
**R_total = V / I**

Then, find the equivalent resistance of the known resistors and subtract it from R_total to get P.

For example, if the supply voltage is 10 V and other resistors have a total equivalent resistance of 6.5 Ω, then:
R_total = 10 / 1 = 10 Ω  
P = R_total - 6.5 = 3.5 Ω

Therefore, the resistance of P is **3.5 Ω**.
```
