**A toaster operating at 240 V has a resistance of 120 Ω. Its power is**

```markdown
Given:
- Voltage, V = 240 V
- Resistance, R = 120 Ω

Power formula:  
P = V² / R

P = (240)² / 120  
P = 57600 / 120  
P = 480 W

Therefore, the power of the toaster is **480 W**.
```
