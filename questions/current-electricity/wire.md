**A wire of resistance 2 ohms per meter is bent to form a circle of radius 1m. The equivalent resistance between its two diametrically opposite points, A and B as shown in the figure is**

![](qns-1.png)

```markdown
Given:
- Resistance per meter = 2 Ω/m
- Radius of the circle, r = 1 m
- Circumference of the circle = 2πr = 2π m

Points A and B are diametrically opposite, so the circle is divided into two equal semicircular halves.

Length of each semicircle = πr = π × 1 = π m

Resistance of each semicircle = length × resistance per meter = π × 2 = 2π Ω

These two semicircles are connected in **parallel** between A and B.

Equivalent resistance:  
1/R_eq = 1/(2π) + 1/(2π) = 2/(2π) = 1/π  
R_eq = π Ω

Therefore, the equivalent resistance between A and B is **π Ω**.
```
