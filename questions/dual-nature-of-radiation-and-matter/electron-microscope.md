**In an electron microscope, the electrons are accelerated by a voltage of 14 kV. If the voltage is changed to 224 kV, then the de Broglie wavelength associated with the electrons would**

```markdown
The de Broglie wavelength (λ) is given by:

λ = h / p = h / √(2meV)

So, λ ∝ 1/√V

Given:
- Initial voltage, V₁ = 14 kV
- Final voltage, V₂ = 224 kV

λ₂ / λ₁ = √(V₁ / V₂) = √(14 / 224) = √(1/16) = 1/4

Therefore, λ₂ = λ₁ / 4 → the wavelength **decreases by 4 times**.

Correct answer: **decrease by 4 times**
```
