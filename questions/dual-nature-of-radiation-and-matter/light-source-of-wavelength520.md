**A light source of wavelength 520 nm emits 1.04 × 10¹⁵ photons per second while the second source of 460 nm produces 1.38 × 10¹⁵ photons per second. Then the ratio of power of second source to that of first source is**

```markdown
Energy of one photon: E = hc/λ

Power of source: P = (number of photons per second) × (energy per photon)

P = n × hc/λ

For first source:  
n₁ = 1.04 × 10¹⁵, λ₁ = 520 nm  
P₁ = n₁ × hc/λ₁

For second source:  
n₂ = 1.38 × 10¹⁵, λ₂ = 460 nm  
P₂ = n₂ × hc/λ₂

Ratio:  
P₂/P₁ = (n₂ × hc/λ₂) / (n₁ × hc/λ₁) = (n₂/n₁) × (λ₁/λ₂)

P₂/P₁ = (1.38 × 10¹⁵ / 1.04 × 10¹⁵) × (520 / 460)  
= (1.3269) × (1.1304) = 1.5

Therefore, P₂/P₁ = 1.5

Correct answer: **1.5**
```
