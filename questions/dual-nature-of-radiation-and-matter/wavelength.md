**The wavelength λₑ of an electron and λₚ of a photon of same energy E are related by**

```markdown
Energy of electron:
E = p²/2m = h²/(2mλₑ²)  (since p = h/λ)

Energy of photon:
E = hc/λₚ

Since both energies are equal:
h²/(2mλₑ²) = hc/λₚ

λₚ = (hc) × (2mλₑ²)/h²
λₚ = (2mc/h) × λₑ²

Therefore, λₚ ∝ λₑ²

Correct answer: **λₚ ∝ λₑ²**
```
