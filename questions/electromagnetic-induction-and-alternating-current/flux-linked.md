**The flux linked with a coil at any instant t is given by ΦB = 10t² - 50t + 250. The induced emf at t = 3 s is**

```markdown
Faraday's law of induction:
ε = -dΦ/dt

Φ = 10t² - 50t + 250

dΦ/dt = 20t - 50

At t = 3 s:
dΦ/dt = 20(3) - 50 = 60 - 50 = 10

ε = -10 V

Therefore, the induced emf at t = 3 s is **−10 V**.

Correct answer: **−10 V**
```
