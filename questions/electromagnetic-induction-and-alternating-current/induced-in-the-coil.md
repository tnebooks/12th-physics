**An electron moves on a straight line path XY as shown in the figure. The coil abcd is adjacent to the path of the electron. What will be the direction of current, if any, induced in the coil?**

![](qns-1.png)

```markdown
An electron carries a negative charge. As it moves, it can be considered as a current in the opposite direction to its motion.

As the electron approaches the coil, the magnetic flux through the coil increases.  
As the electron moves past and goes away from the coil, the flux decreases.

Due to Lenz's law, the induced current will oppose the change in flux.

- When the electron approaches: current flows in one direction (say abcd)
- When the electron moves away: current flows in the opposite direction (adcb)

Therefore, **the current will reverse its direction as the electron goes past the coil**.

Correct answer: **The current will reverse its direction as the electron goes past the coil**
```
