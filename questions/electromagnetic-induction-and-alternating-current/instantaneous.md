**The instantaneous values of alternating current and voltage in a circuit are i = 1/√2 sin(100πt) A and v = 1/√2 sin[100πt + π/3] V. The average power in watts consumed in the circuit is**

```markdown
Given:
- i = I₀ sin(ωt) → I₀ = 1/√2 A, I_rms = I₀/√2 = (1/√2)/√2 = 1/2 A
- v = V₀ sin(ωt + φ) → V₀ = 1/√2 V, V_rms = V₀/√2 = (1/√2)/√2 = 1/2 V
- Phase difference, φ = π/3

Average power: P = V_rms × I_rms × cos φ

cos φ = cos(π/3) = 1/2

P = (1/2) × (1/2) × (1/2) = 1/8 W

Therefore, the average power consumed in the circuit is **1/8 W**.

Correct answer: **1/8**
```
