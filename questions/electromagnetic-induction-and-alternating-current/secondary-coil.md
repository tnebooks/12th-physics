**In a transformer, the number of turns in the primary and the secondary are 410 and 1230 respectively. If the current in primary is 6A, then that in the secondary coil is**

```markdown
In a transformer, the current is inversely proportional to the number of turns:

Iₚ / Iₛ = Nₛ / Nₚ

Given:
- Nₚ = 410 (primary turns)
- Nₛ = 1230 (secondary turns)
- Iₚ = 6 A (primary current)

Iₛ = Iₚ × (Nₚ / Nₛ) = 6 × (410 / 1230) = 6 × (1/3) = 2 A

Therefore, the current in the secondary coil is **2 A**.

Correct answer: **2 A**
```
