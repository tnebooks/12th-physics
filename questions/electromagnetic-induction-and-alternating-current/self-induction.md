**When the current changes from +2A to −2A in 0.05 s, an emf of 8 V is induced in a coil. The co-efficient of self-induction of the coil is**

```markdown
Induced emf: ε = -L (dI/dt)

Given:
- ε = 8 V (induced emf)
- ΔI = -2 - (+2) = -4 A (change in current)
- Δt = 0.05 s

dI/dt = ΔI/Δt = -4 / 0.05 = -80 A/s

ε = -L (dI/dt)
8 = -L × (-80) = 80L
L = 8/80 = 0.1 H

Therefore, the coefficient of self-induction of the coil is **0.1 H**.

Correct answer: **0.1 H**
```
