**A thin semi-circular conducting ring (PQR) of radius r is falling with its plane vertical in a horizontal magnetic field B, as shown in the figure. The potential difference developed across the ring when its speed v, is**

![](qns-2.png)

```markdown
When a conductor moves in a magnetic field, an emf is induced.

For a straight conductor moving perpendicular to the magnetic field:
ε = B l v

Here, the effective length between P and R (the two ends of the semicircle) is the diameter = 2r.

Therefore, the induced emf: ε = B × (2r) × v = 2rBv

Due to the magnetic force on charges, the end R will be at a higher potential (depending on the direction of motion and magnetic field orientation).

Therefore, the potential difference developed is **2rBv and R is at higher potential**.

Correct answer: **2rBv and R is at higher potential**
```
