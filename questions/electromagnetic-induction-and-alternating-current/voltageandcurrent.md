**In a series RL circuit, the resistance and inductive reactance are the same. The phase difference between voltage and current in the circuit is**

```markdown
In a series RL circuit, the phase difference (φ) between voltage and current is given by:

tan φ = X_L / R

Given that the resistance (R) and inductive reactance (X_L) are equal:

X_L = R

tan φ = R / R = 1

φ = tan⁻¹(1) = π/4

Therefore, the phase difference between voltage and current is **π/4**.

Correct answer: **π/4**
```
