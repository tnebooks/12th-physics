**If the amplitude of the magnetic field is 3 × 10⁻⁶ T, then amplitude of the electric field for a electromagnetic waves is**

```markdown
For an electromagnetic wave, the relationship between the electric field (E) and magnetic field (B) is:

E = c × B

where c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s (speed of light)

Given: B = 3 × 10⁻⁶ T

E = 3 × 10⁸ × 3 × 10⁻⁶
E = 9 × 10² = 900 V m⁻¹

Therefore, the amplitude of the electric field is **900 V m⁻¹**.

Correct answer: **900 V m⁻¹**
```
