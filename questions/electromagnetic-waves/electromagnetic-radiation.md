**Which of the following electromagnetic radiations is used for viewing objects through fog**

```markdown
**Infrared rays** are used for viewing objects through fog and smoke.

Reason: Infrared radiation has longer wavelengths than visible light, so it is scattered less by fog particles and can penetrate through fog, mist, and smoke more effectively.

This property is utilized in:
- Night vision devices
- Thermal imaging cameras
- Fog-penetrating surveillance systems

Therefore, the correct answer is **infrared**.
```
