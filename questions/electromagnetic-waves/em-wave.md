**An e.m. wave is propagating in a medium with a velocity v = vî. The instantaneous oscillating electric field of this e.m. wave is along +y-axis, then the direction of oscillating magnetic field of the e.m. wave will be along:**

```markdown
For an electromagnetic wave, the propagation direction is given by the cross product of the electric field (E) and magnetic field (B) vectors: v ∝ E × B.

Here, propagation is along +x (î), and E is along +y (ĵ). So, î = ĵ × B. Solving for B, we get B along +z (k̂), because ĵ × k̂ = î.

Therefore, the correct answer is +z direction.
```
