**Fraunhofer lines are an example of _______ spectrum.**

```markdown
Fraunhofer lines are the dark lines observed in the solar spectrum.

They are formed when the light emitted by the Sun's core passes through the cooler outer atmosphere (chromosphere), where certain elements absorb specific wavelengths of light.

This results in dark lines on a continuous bright background, which is characteristic of a **line absorption spectrum**.

Therefore, the correct answer is **line absorption**.
```
