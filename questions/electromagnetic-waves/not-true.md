**Which of the following is NOT true for electromagnetic waves?**

```markdown
Electromagnetic waves transport energy, momentum, and also angular momentum (due to polarization). However, in vacuum, all electromagnetic waves travel at the same speed (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s), regardless of their frequency. The statement "in vacuum, it travels with different speeds which depend on their frequency" is false because speed in vacuum is constant for all frequencies. Therefore, the correct answer is "in vacuum, it travels with different speeds which depend on their frequency."
```
