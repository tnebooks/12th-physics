**The electric and magnetic fields of an electromagnetic wave are**

```markdown
In an electromagnetic wave, the electric field (E) and magnetic field (B) are in phase with each other. They are also perpendicular to each other and to the direction of wave propagation. Therefore, the correct answer is "in phase and perpendicular to each other."
```
