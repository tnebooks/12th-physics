**Which one of them is used to produce a propagating electromagnetic wave?**

```markdown
Electromagnetic waves are produced only by accelerating charged particles. A charge moving with constant velocity, a stationary charge, or an uncharged particle do not radiate electromagnetic waves. When a charged particle accelerates, it produces changing electric and magnetic fields, which propagate as electromagnetic waves. Therefore, the correct answer is "an accelerating charge."
```
