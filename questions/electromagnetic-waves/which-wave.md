**Which of the following is an electromagnetic wave?**

```markdown
α - rays are streams of helium nuclei (He²⁺) — they are not electromagnetic waves.  
β - rays are streams of electrons or positrons — they are also not electromagnetic waves.  
γ - rays are high-energy electromagnetic radiation, just like light, X-rays, etc.  
Therefore, the only electromagnetic wave among the options is γ - rays.  
So, the correct answer is "γ - rays."
```
