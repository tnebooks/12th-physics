**The barrier potential of a p-n junction depends on i) type of semiconductor material ii) amount of doping iii) temperature. Which one of the following is correct?**

```markdown
The barrier potential (built-in potential) of a p-n junction depends on:
- Type of semiconductor material (e.g., Si, Ge) — affects the band gap and intrinsic carrier concentration.
- Amount of doping — higher doping increases the built-in potential.
- Temperature — as temperature increases, the built-in potential decreases.

Therefore, all three factors — (i), (ii), and (iii) — affect the barrier potential. Hence, the correct answer is "(i) (ii) and (iii)".
```
