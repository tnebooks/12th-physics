**To obtain sustained oscillation in an oscillator,**

```markdown
To obtain sustained (undamped) oscillations in an oscillator, the **Barkhausen criterion** must be satisfied. The two conditions are:

1. **Feedback should be positive** — The feedback signal must be in phase with the input signal.
2. **Feedback factor (β) must be unity** — The loop gain (Aβ) must be equal to 1, i.e., |Aβ| = 1.
3. **Phase shift must be 0 or 2π** — The total phase shift around the loop must be 0° (or 360°), meaning the feedback is positive.

Since all three conditions are required for sustained oscillations, the correct answer is **"All the above"**.
```
