**The principle based on which a solar cell operates is**

```markdown
A solar cell operates on the principle of the **Photovoltaic effect**.

In the photovoltaic effect:
- When photons from sunlight strike the p-n junction of a semiconductor material, electron-hole pairs are generated.
- These charge carriers are separated by the electric field at the junction, creating a voltage difference.
- This results in the generation of direct current (DC) electricity.

Other options:
- **"Diffusion"** → Refers to the movement of carriers from high concentration to low concentration (occurs in a p-n junction but is not the operating principle of a solar cell).
- **"Recombination"** → This is the principle behind LEDs (light emission), not solar cells.
- **"Carrier flow"** → General term for current flow, not the specific principle of a solar cell.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"Photovoltaic action"**.
```
