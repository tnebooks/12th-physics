**If a small amount of antimony (Sb) is added to germanium crystal,**

```markdown
Antimony (Sb) is a **pentavalent** (group 5) element. When added to germanium (Ge) (group 4) crystal:

- Four of Sb's electrons form covalent bonds with Ge atoms, leaving the fifth electron as a **free electron**.
- This increases the number of free electrons compared to holes.
- The semiconductor becomes **n-type** (not p-type).
- Antimony acts as a **donor** atom (not an acceptor).
- The resistance **decreases** (not increases) due to increased charge carriers.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"there will be more free electrons than holes in the semiconductor"**.
```
