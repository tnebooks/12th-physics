**In an unbiased p-n junction, the majority charge carriers (that is, holes) in the p-region diffuse into n-region because of**

```markdown
In an unbiased p-n junction, diffusion occurs due to the **concentration gradient** of charge carriers across the junction.

- In the p-region, holes are the majority carriers and their concentration is very high.
- In the n-region, holes are the minority carriers and their concentration is very low.
- This difference in concentration (higher in p-region, lower in n-region) causes the holes to diffuse from the p-region to the n-region.

Other options:
- **"the potential difference across the p-n junction"** → This is the result of diffusion, not the cause. The potential barrier (built-in voltage) actually opposes further diffusion.
- **"the attraction of free electrons of n-region"** → Holes are not attracted by electrons; diffusion is driven by concentration difference, not electrostatic attraction.
- **"All of the above"** → Incorrect, since the first two are not the cause.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"the higher hole concentration in p-region than that in n-region"**.
```
