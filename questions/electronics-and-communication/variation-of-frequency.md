**The variation of frequency of carrier wave with respect to the amplitude of the modulating signal is called**

```markdown
When the **frequency** of the carrier wave is varied in accordance with the instantaneous amplitude of the modulating signal, it is called **Frequency Modulation (FM)**.

Other types of modulation:
- **Amplitude Modulation (AM)** → The amplitude of the carrier wave is varied, while frequency remains constant.
- **Phase Modulation (PM)** → The phase of the carrier wave is varied.
- **Pulse Width Modulation (PWM)** → The width of the pulse is varied.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"Frequency modulation"**.
```
