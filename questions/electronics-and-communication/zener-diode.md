**The zener diode is primarily used as**

```markdown
A **Zener diode** is primarily used as a **voltage regulator**. It operates in the **reverse breakdown region**, where it maintains a nearly constant voltage across its terminals even when the current through it varies significantly.

Other options:
- **Rectifier** → This is the primary function of a regular p-n junction diode (e.g., half-wave or full-wave rectifier).
- **Oscillator** → Used to generate continuous waveforms; typically uses transistors or ICs, not Zener diodes.
- **Amplifier** → Used to increase signal strength; uses transistors or operational amplifiers, not Zener diodes.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"Voltage regulator"**.
```
