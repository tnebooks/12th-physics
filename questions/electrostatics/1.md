Which charge configuration produces a uniform electric field?

```markdown
The electric field due to a uniformly charged infinite plane sheet is given by $E = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$, which is independent of distance $r$, thereby producing a uniform electric field everywhere.
```
