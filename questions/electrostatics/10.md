If the Velocity and Wavelength of light in air is $V_a$ and $\lambda_a$ and that in water is $V_w$ and $\lambda_w$, then the refractive index of water is:

```markdown
The refractive index of a medium relative to air is defined as $\mu = \frac{\text{Speed of light in air}}{\text{Speed of light in medium}} = \frac{V_a}{V_w}$.
```
