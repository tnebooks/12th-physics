The internal resistance of a 2.1 V cell which gives a current of 0.2 A through a resistance of 10 Ω is:

```markdown
Using $I = \frac{E}{R + r} \implies 0.2 = \frac{2.1}{10 + r} \implies 2 + 0.2r = 2.1 \implies 0.2r = 0.1 \implies r = 0.5\ \Omega$.
```
