A $1\text{ kg}$ ball moving at $4\text{ m/s}$ collides and sticks to a stationary $1\text{ kg}$ ball. What is their final velocity?

```latex
m_1 v_1 = (m_1 + m_2) v_f \implies (1)(4) = (2) v_f \implies v_f = 2\text{ m/s}
```
