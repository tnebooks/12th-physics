Two charges of $+2\mu\text{C}$ and $+3\mu\text{C}$ are $1\text{ m}$ apart. Calculate the force ($k=9\times 10^9$).

```latex
F = k \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2} = 9\times 10^9 \frac{(2\times 10^{-6})(3\times 10^{-6})}{1^2} = 0.054\text{ N}
```
