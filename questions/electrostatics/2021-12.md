An ideal gas at constant pressure expands to double its volume. What happens to its Kelvin temperature?

```latex
\frac{V_1}{T_1} = \frac{V_2}{T_2} \implies T_2 = T_1 \frac{2V_1}{V_1} = 2T_1
```
