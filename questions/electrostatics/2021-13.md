Calculate the energy of a photon with frequency $f = 5\times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$ ($h=6.63\times 10^{-34}\text{ J}\cdot\text{s}$).

```latex
E = h f = (6.63\times 10^{-34})(5\times 10^{14}) = 3.315\times 10^{-19}\text{ J}
```
