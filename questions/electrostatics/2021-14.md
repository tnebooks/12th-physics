A sample has a half-life of $10\text{ days}$. What fraction remains after $30\text{ days}$?

```latex
\text{Fraction} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{30/10} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{8}
```
