Calculate the rest energy equivalent of a mass $m = 0.001\text{ kg}$ ($c=3\times 10^8\text{ m/s}$).

```latex
E = m c^2 = (0.001)(3\times 10^8)^2 = 10^{-3} \times (9\times 10^{16}) = 9\times 10^{13}\text{ J}
```
