The unit of electric flux is:

```markdown
Electric flux is defined as $\Phi_E = E \cdot A$. The unit of electric field $E$ is $\text{N/C}$ and area $A$ is $\text{m}^2$, giving the SI unit $\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2\text{C}^{-1}$.
```
