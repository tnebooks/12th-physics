answers:
  - "more than before"

Two identical conducting balls having positive charges $ q_1 $ and $ q_2 $ are separated by a centre to centre distance $ r $. If they are made to touch each other and then separated to the same distance, the force between them will be  
(NSEP 04-05)


```markdown
Initial force:
$$
F_i = \frac{k q_1 q_2}{r^2}
$$

When they touch, charge redistributes equally (identical conductors):
$$
q' = \frac{q_1 + q_2}{2}
$$

Final force:
$$
F_f = \frac{k (q')^2}{r^2} = \frac{k}{r^2} \left(\frac{q_1 + q_2}{2}\right)^2
$$

Compare:
$$
F_f - F_i = \frac{k}{r^2} \left[ \frac{(q_1+q_2)^2}{4} - q_1q_2 \right] = \frac{k}{r^2} \left[ \frac{q_1^2 + 2q_1q_2 + q_2^2 - 4q_1q_2}{4} \right]
$$
$$
= \frac{k}{r^2} \left[ \frac{(q_1 - q_2)^2}{4} \right] > 0
$$

Since $ (q_1 - q_2)^2 > 0 $ (unless equal), final force is **greater** than initial force.

Therefore, ** more than before** is correct.
```
