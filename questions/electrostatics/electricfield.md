An electric field E =10xî exists in a certain region of space. Then the potential difference V = Vo – VA, where Vo is the potential at the origin and VA is the potential at x = 2 m is:

```markdown


### Explanation:

The electric field is given as:

$$
\mathbf{E} = 10x \, \hat{\mathbf{i}}
$$

The general relation between electric field and potential is:

$$
\mathbf{E} = -\nabla V
$$

For one dimension (x-axis):

$$
E_x = -\frac{dV}{dx}
$$

To find the potential difference between the origin (O, at x = 0) and point A (at x = 2 m):

$$
V_O - V_A = -\int_{A}^{O} E_x \, dx
$$

Changing the limits (from O to A):

$$
V_O - V_A = \int_{0}^{2} E_x \, dx
$$

Substitute \(E_x = 10x\):

$$
V_O - V_A = \int_{0}^{2} 10x \, dx
= \left[5x^2\right]_{0}^{2}
= 5(4) - 0
= 20 \, \text{V}
$$

---



$$
\boxed{+20 \, \text{V}}
$$


```
