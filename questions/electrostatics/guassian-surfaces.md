**Four Gaussian surfaces are given below with charges inside each Gaussian surface. Rank the electric flux through each Gaussian surface in increasing order.**

![](qns-5.png)

```markdown
According to Gauss's law, electric flux (Φ) through a closed surface is given by:
Φ = Q_enc / ε₀

where Q_enc is the net charge enclosed by the Gaussian surface.

The flux depends only on the net enclosed charge, not on the size or shape of the surface.

For the given surfaces (assuming the charges inside are such that):
- Surface A encloses the largest positive net charge
- Surface B encloses a smaller positive net charge
- Surface C encloses an even smaller positive net charge
- Surface D encloses the smallest net charge (or possibly negative, making flux least)

Thus, the increasing order of electric flux (smallest to largest) is:
D < C < B < A

Therefore, the correct answer is **D < C < B < A**.
```
