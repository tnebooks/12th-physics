The wavelength λe of an electron and λp of a photon of same energy E are related by:

```markdown
For an electron, E ∝ 1/λe² (non-relativistically), while for a photon E ∝ 1/λp. For the same energy, 1/λp ∝ 1/λe², hence λp ∝ λe².
```
