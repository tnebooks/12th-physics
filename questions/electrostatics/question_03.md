If the magnitude of the magnetic field is 3 × 10^-6 T, then the magnitude of the electric field for an electromagnetic wave is:

```markdown
For an electromagnetic wave, E = cB. Hence E = (3 × 10^8)(3 × 10^-6) = 900 V m^-1.
```
