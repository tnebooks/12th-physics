A carbon resistor of 47 ± 4.7 kΩ is to be marked with rings of different colours for its identification. The colour code sequence will be:

```markdown
47 kΩ = 47 × 10³ Ω. Digits 4 and 7 correspond to Yellow and Violet; multiplier 10³ corresponds to Orange. A ±10% tolerance corresponds to Silver.
```
