A wire of length l carrying a current I along the Y direction is kept in a magnetic field given by B = (β/√3)(î + ĵ + k̂) T. The magnitude of Lorentz force acting on the wire is:

```markdown
The force magnitude is |F| = IlB sin θ. The field component perpendicular to the Y direction has magnitude β√(2/3), giving F = √(2/3) βIl.
```
