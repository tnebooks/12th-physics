The electric potential of an electron is given by V = V₀ ln(r/r₀), where r₀ is a constant. If Bohr atom model is valid, then variation of radius of nth orbit rₙ with the quantum number n is:

```markdown
For V ∝ ln r, the electric field varies as 1/r, giving a central force proportional to 1/r. Combining circular motion with Bohr quantization gives rₙ proportional to n.
```
