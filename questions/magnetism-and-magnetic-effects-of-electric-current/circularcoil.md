**A circular coil of radius 5 cm and 50 turns carries a current of 3 ampere. The magnetic dipole moment of the coil is nearly**

```markdown
The magnetic dipole moment (m) of a coil is given by:

m = N × I × A

Where:
- N = number of turns = 50
- I = current = 3 A
- A = area of the coil = πr²

Given:
- Radius r = 5 cm = 5 × 10⁻² m

A = π × (5 × 10⁻²)² = π × 25 × 10⁻⁴ = 25π × 10⁻⁴ m²

Now, calculate m:
m = 50 × 3 × (25π × 10⁻⁴)
m = 150 × 25π × 10⁻⁴
m = 3750π × 10⁻⁴
m = 3.75π × 10⁻¹
m = 1.178 × 10⁻¹ ≈ 1.18 A m²

Rounding off to one decimal place:
m ≈ 1.2 A m²

Therefore, the magnetic dipole moment of the coil is nearly **1.2 A m²**.
```
