**An electron moves in a straight line inside a charged parallel plate capacitor of uniform charge density σ. The time taken by the electron to cross the parallel plate capacitor undeflected when the plates of the capacitor are kept under constant magnetic field of induction (B) is**

![](qns-2.png)

```markdown
For the electron to move undeflected in crossed electric and magnetic fields, the electric force and magnetic force must balance each other:

qE = qvB  →  v = E/B

For a parallel plate capacitor with uniform surface charge density σ:
Electric field E = σ/ε₀

So, velocity of electron: v = (σ/ε₀)/B = σ/(ε₀B)

The electron crosses the capacitor of length l (length of plates):
Time taken t = distance/velocity = l / v = l / (σ/(ε₀B)) = ε₀ l B / σ

Therefore, the time taken by the electron to cross the capacitor undeflected is **ɛ₀ lB/σ**.

Note: The mass and charge of the electron do not appear in the final expression because they cancel out when equating electric and magnetic forces (qE = qvB).
```
