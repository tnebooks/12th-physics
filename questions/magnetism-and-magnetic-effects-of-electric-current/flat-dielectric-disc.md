**A flat dielectric disc of radius R carries an excess charge on its surface. The surface charge density is σ. The disc rotates about an axis perpendicular to its plane passing through the centre with angular velocity ω. Find the magnitude of the torque on the disc if it is placed in a uniform magnetic field whose strength is B which is directed perpendicular to the axis of rotation?**

```markdown
When a charged disc rotates, it constitutes a current loop. The magnetic dipole moment (m) of the rotating charged disc is given by:

m = (1/4) σ ω π R⁴

The torque (τ) experienced by a magnetic dipole in a uniform magnetic field B is:

τ = m × B = mB sinθ

Here, θ = 90° (since B is perpendicular to the axis of rotation, and m is along the axis), so sinθ = 1.

Therefore, τ = mB = (1/4) σ ω π R⁴ × B

τ = (1/4) σ ω π B R⁴

So, the magnitude of the torque on the disc is **1/4 σωπBR⁴**.

Note: If the magnetic field were parallel to the axis, the torque would be zero. But here it is perpendicular, so torque is maximum.
```
