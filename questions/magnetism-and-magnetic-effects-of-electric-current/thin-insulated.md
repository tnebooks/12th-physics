**A thin insulated wire forms a plane spiral of N = 100 tight turns carrying a current I = 8 mA (milli ampere). The radii of inside and outside turns are a = 50 mm and b = 100 mm respectively. The magnetic induction at the centre of the spiral is**

```markdown
For a plane spiral coil, the magnetic field at the centre is given by:

B = (μ₀ N I) / (2(b - a)) × ln(b/a)

Where:
- N = 100 turns
- I = 8 mA = 8 × 10⁻³ A
- a = 50 mm = 0.05 m (inner radius)
- b = 100 mm = 0.10 m (outer radius)
- μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T m/A

ln(b/a) = ln(0.10/0.05) = ln(2) = 0.693

Now, calculate:

B = (4π × 10⁻⁷ × 100 × 8 × 10⁻³) / (2 × (0.10 - 0.05)) × 0.693

B = (4π × 10⁻⁷ × 0.8) / (0.10) × 0.693

B = (4π × 8 × 10⁻⁸) / 0.1 × 0.693

B = (32π × 10⁻⁸) / 0.1 × 0.693

B = (32 × 3.1416 × 10⁻⁸) × 10 × 0.693

B = (1.0053 × 10⁻⁶) × 6.93

B = 6.967 × 10⁻⁶ T ≈ 7 × 10⁻⁶ T = 7 μT

Therefore, the magnetic induction at the centre of the spiral is **7 μT**.
```
