**A ray of light travelling in a transparent medium of refractive index n falls, on a surface separating the medium from air at an angle of incidents of 45°. The ray can undergo total internal reflection for the following n,**

```markdown
For **Total Internal Reflection (TIR)** to occur, the angle of incidence ($i$) must be greater than or equal to the critical angle ($i_c$).
$$i \ge i_c$$

Given that the angle of incidence $i = 45^\circ$, we have:
$$45^\circ \ge i_c$$
$$\sin(45^\circ) \ge \sin(i_c)$$

The formula for the critical angle when light travels from a medium of refractive index $n$ to air (refractive index $\approx 1$) is:
$$\sin(i_c) = \frac{1}{n}$$

Substituting the values into the inequality:
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \ge \frac{1}{n}$$
$$n \ge \sqrt{2}$$

We know that $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$. Therefore, for TIR to take place, the refractive index of the medium must be greater than $1.414$.

Looking at the given options, the only value greater than $1.414$ is **1.5**.

Correct answer is **n = 1.5**.

```
