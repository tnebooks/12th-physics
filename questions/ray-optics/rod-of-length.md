**A rod of length 10 cm lies along the principal axis of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm in such a way that its end closer to the pole is 20 cm away from the mirror. The length of the image is,**

```markdown
For a concave mirror, the focal length is $f = -10\text{ cm}$ (by sign convention).

The rod has a length of $10\text{ cm}$, and its closer end is at $20\text{ cm}$ from the pole. Since $20\text{ cm} = 2f$, this end is exactly at the center of curvature ($C$). The farther end of the rod extends away from the mirror to a distance of $20\text{ cm} + 10\text{ cm} = 30\text{ cm}$.

Let's locate the image positions for both ends of the rod using the mirror formula:
$$\frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$$

**1. For the closer end (End 1):**
* Object distance, $u_1 = -20\text{ cm}$
$$\frac{1}{v_1} + \frac{1}{-20} = \frac{1}{-10}$$
$$\frac{1}{v_1} = \frac{1}{20} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{1 - 2}{20} = -\frac{1}{20}$$
$$v_1 = -20\text{ cm}$$
*(Since End 1 is at the center of curvature $C$, its image is also formed at $C$).*



**2. For the farther end (End 2):**
* Object distance, $u_2 = -30\text{ cm}$
$$\frac{1}{v_2} + \frac{1}{-30} = \frac{1}{-10}$$
$$\frac{1}{v_2} = \frac{1}{30} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{1 - 3}{30} = -\frac{2}{30} = -\frac{1}{15}$$
$$v_2 = -15\text{ cm}$$

**Length of the Image:**
The length of the image is the absolute difference between the image positions of its two ends:
$$\text{Length of image} = |v_2 - v_1|$$
$$\text{Length of image} = |-15 - (-20)| = |-15 + 20| = 5\text{ cm}$$

Therefore, the total length of the image formed is **5 cm**.

```
