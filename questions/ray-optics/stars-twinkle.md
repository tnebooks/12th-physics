**Stars twinkle due to,**

```markdown
Stars twinkle primarily due to **atmospheric refraction** of star light. 

When starlight enters the Earth's atmosphere, it passes through multiple layers of air with varying densities and temperatures. Since these layers are constantly moving and changing due to winds and thermal currents, the refractive index of the air keeps fluctuating continuously.



As a result:
* The apparent position of the star shifts slightly from moment to moment.
* The amount of starlight reaching our eyes flickers, making the star appear brighter at one instant and dimmer at another.

This continuous variation in position and brightness creates the twinkling effect.

Correct answer is **refraction**.

```
