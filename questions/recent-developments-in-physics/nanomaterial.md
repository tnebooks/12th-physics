**Which one of the following is the natural nanomaterial.**

```markdown
**Peacock feather** is a natural nanomaterial. The vibrant colors of a peacock feather are not due to pigments but due to **structural coloration** caused by photonic crystals — periodic nanostructures that selectively reflect light of specific wavelengths. These nanostructures are natural examples of nanomaterials.

Other options:
- **"Peacock beak"** → Made of keratin and bone; not primarily nanostructured.
- **"Grain of sand"** → Microscopic but typically in the micrometer range, not nanoscale (though some sand grains may contain nanoparticles, they are not considered nanomaterials themselves).
- **"Skin of the Whale"** → Biological tissue; not specifically a nanomaterial.

Natural nanomaterials are materials found in nature with at least one dimension in the nanoscale (1–100 nm). Peacock feathers, lotus leaves, butterfly wings, and gecko feet are classic examples.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"Peacock feather"**.
```
