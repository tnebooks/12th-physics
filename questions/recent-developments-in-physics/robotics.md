**The materials used in Robotics are**

```markdown
The materials widely used in robotics are **"Steel and aluminium"**.

- **Steel** — Used for structural frames, gears, and actuators due to its high strength, durability, and wear resistance.
- **Aluminium** — Used for lightweight components, robotic arms, and mobile robot bodies due to its low density, good strength-to-weight ratio, and corrosion resistance.

Other options:
- **"Aluminium and silver"** → Silver is expensive and used primarily for electrical contacts, not as a structural material in robotics.
- **"Silver and gold"** → Both are precious metals; used in electronics (contacts, sensors) but not as primary structural materials in robotics.
- **"Copper and gold"** → Copper is used for wiring and motors; gold for connectors and sensors. However, steel and aluminium are the primary structural materials.

Modern robotics also uses composites, plastics, and shape memory alloys, but the most common structural materials remain steel and aluminium.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"Steel and aluminum"**.
```
