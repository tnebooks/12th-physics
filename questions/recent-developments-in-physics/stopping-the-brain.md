```markdown
The technology used for stopping the brain from processing pain (by distracting or reducing pain perception) is **"Virtual Reality" (VR)**.

- VR technology immerses patients in a virtual environment, diverting their attention away from painful stimuli.
- This is known as **VR pain management** and is used for burn victims, cancer patients, chronic pain, and during painful medical procedures.
- VR has been shown to reduce activity in pain-processing regions of the brain.

Other options:
- **"Precision medicine"** → Personalized treatment based on genetics and patient-specific factors; not used to stop pain processing directly.
- **"Wireless brain sensor"** → Used to record brain activity (EEG), but not to stop pain processing.
- **"Radiology"** → Uses imaging techniques like X-ray, MRI, CT scans for diagnosis; does not stop pain processing.

Therefore, the correct answer is **"Virtual reality"**.
```
