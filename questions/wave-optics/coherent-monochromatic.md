Two coherent monochromatic light beams of intensities I and 4I are superposed. The maximum and minimum possible intensities in the resulting beam are

```markdown
The maximum and minimum intensities produced by the interference of two coherent light beams are given by

$$
I_{\max} = (\sqrt{I_1} + \sqrt{I_2})^2
$$

and

$$
I_{\min} = (\sqrt{I_1} - \sqrt{I_2})^2
$$

Given

$$
I_1 = I,\qquad I_2 = 4I
$$

For maximum intensity,

$$
I_{\max} = (\sqrt{I} + 2\sqrt{I})^2 = (3\sqrt{I})^2 = 9I
$$

For minimum intensity,

$$
I_{\min} = (2\sqrt{I} - \sqrt{I})^2 = (\sqrt{I})^2 = I
$$

Hence, **the maximum and minimum possible intensities are $9I$ and $I$, respectively.**
```
