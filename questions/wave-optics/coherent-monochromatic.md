**Two coherent monochromatic light beams of intensities I and 4I are superposed. The maximum and minimum possible intensities in the resulting beam are**


```markdown

When two coherent light beams interfere, the maximum and minimum intensities are given by:

$$I_{\text{max}} = (\sqrt{I_1} + \sqrt{I_2})^2$$

$$I_{\text{min}} = (\sqrt{I_1} - \sqrt{I_2})^2$$

Given:

* $I_1 = I$
* $I_2 = 4I$

$$I_{\text{max}} = (\sqrt{I} + \sqrt{4I})^2 = (\sqrt{I} + 2\sqrt{I})^2 = (3\sqrt{I})^2 = 9I$$

$$I_{\text{min}} = (\sqrt{I} - \sqrt{4I})^2 = (\sqrt{I} - 2\sqrt{I})^2 = (-\sqrt{I})^2 = I$$

Therefore, the maximum intensity is $9I$ and the minimum intensity is $I$.

Correct answer: **$9I$ and $I$**.
```
