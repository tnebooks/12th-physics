A plane glass is placed over a various coloured letters (violet, green, yellow, red) The letter which appears to be raised more is,

```markdown
When viewed through a plane glass slab, objects appear raised due to refraction. The apparent shift is greater for light with a higher refractive index.

Since the refractive index of glass is highest for violet light and lowest for red light, violet light undergoes the maximum apparent shift.

Hence, **the violet letter appears to be raised more.**
```
