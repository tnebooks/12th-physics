**A plane glass is placed over a various coloured letters (violet, green, yellow, red) The letter which appears to be raised more is,**

```markdown

When a plane glass plate is placed over coloured letters, the letters appear to be raised due to refraction. The apparent shift (apparent depth) depends on the refractive index of the glass for different colours.

The apparent shift is given by:

$$\text{Shift} = t \left(1 - \frac{1}{\mu}\right)$$

where $t$ is the thickness of the glass plate and $\mu$ is the refractive index.

Since **refractive index is higher for violet light** (shorter wavelength) compared to red light (longer wavelength), the apparent shift is **maximum for violet**. Therefore, the violet letter appears to be raised the most.

Order of refractive index:

$$\mu_{\text{violet}} > \mu_{\text{green}} > \mu_{\text{yellow}} > \mu_{\text{red}}$$

Order of apparent shift:

$$\text{Violet} > \text{Green} > \text{Yellow} > \text{Red}$$

Therefore, the letter that appears to be raised more is **violet**.

Correct answer: **violet**.
```
