When light is incident on a soap film of thickness 5×10^–5 cm, the wavelength of light reflected maximum in the visible region is 5320 Å. Refractive index of the film will be,

```markdown
For maximum reflection in a thin soap film,

$$
2\mu t = n\lambda
$$

For the first visible maximum, take

$$
n = 1
$$

Given,

$$
t = 5 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{cm}
$$

and

$$
\lambda = 5320\ \text{Å} = 5.32 \times 10^{-5}\ \text{cm}
$$

Substituting,

$$
2\mu (5 \times 10^{-5}) = 5.32 \times 10^{-5}
$$

$$
10\mu = 13.3
$$

$$
\mu = 1.33
$$

Hence, **the refractive index of the soap film is $1.33$.**
```
