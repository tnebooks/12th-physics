One of the of Young’s double slits is covered with a glass plate as shown in figure. The position of central maximum will,

```markdown
When a thin glass plate is placed in front of one slit, the optical path length of light from that slit increases.

This introduces an additional phase difference between the two waves reaching the screen.

As a result, the entire interference pattern shifts toward the slit covered by the glass plate.

Hence, **the position of the central maximum gets shifted upwards.**
```
